How It Works

Three steps. Endless debates.

1

Upload your clip

Post a 10-30 second clip of you playing. Skills, match footage, training — anything goes.

2

The world guesses

Other players watch and pick your level: rec, high school, academy, college, semi-pro, or pro.

3

The reveal hits

After guessing, the player's real level drops. Were you right? Or did you just get exposed?
